# Per Diem Rates by Region

## 1. When Per Diem Applies

Per diem is a flat daily allowance covering meals and incidentals, used instead of itemized meal reimbursement (see Expense Reimbursement Guide) for trips of 3 or more consecutive days, to simplify expense reporting on longer trips.

## 2. Current Rates (Per Day)

| Region | Per Diem |
| --- | --- |
| Western Europe | €70 |
| Eastern Europe | €55 |
| United Kingdom | €75 |
| North America | €85 |
| Rest of World | €60 |

Rates are reviewed annually by Finance and adjusted for inflation and cost-of-living changes; the rates above are the currently published figures.

## 3. What's Included

Per diem covers breakfast, lunch, dinner, and minor incidentals (tips, laundry on long trips). It does not cover accommodation, transport, or client entertainment, which are claimed separately under the Travel Policy and Expense Reimbursement Guide.

## 4. Partial Days

Travel days (departure and return) are paid at 75% of the full per diem rate, reflecting that you're not away for a full 24 hours.

## 5. Per Diem vs. Itemized — Can I Choose?

For trips of 3+ days, per diem is the default and simpler option. You may choose itemized reimbursement instead if your actual costs are expected to be significantly higher (e.g., an expensive client dinner), but you cannot mix both methods for the same day.

## 6. Meals Included in Conference Registration

If meals are already included in a conference ticket or hotel package, deduct the relevant meal(s) from your per diem claim for that day rather than claiming the full rate.

## 7. Currency and Payment

Per diem is calculated in EUR using the rates above regardless of local currency, and paid together with your next expense reimbursement cycle, not in cash in advance.

## 8. Questions on Rates

Rate questions or requests for a region not listed go to Finance, who can confirm the appropriate rate before you travel.
