# Procurement & Vendor Approval Policy

## 1. When This Applies

Any new vendor relationship or purchase order over €1,000, recurring or one-off, must go through procurement review before a commitment is made to the vendor — this is separate from the Software Request & License Policy, which covers software specifically.

## 2. Vendor Due Diligence

Before onboarding a new vendor, Finance and Legal review: basic financial stability, data handling practices (if the vendor will touch any personal data, this also triggers a Data Privacy & GDPR review), and whether a Conflict of Interest exists with the requesting employee (see Conflict of Interest Policy).

## 3. Approval Thresholds

| Purchase Value | Approval Required |
| --- | --- |
| Under €1,000 | Manager only |
| €1,000-€10,000 | Manager + Finance |
| €10,000-€50,000 | Department Head + Finance |
| Over €50,000 | Executive team sign-off |

## 4. Existing vs. New Vendors

Repeat purchases from an already-approved vendor within the same category only need the relevant spend-threshold approval above, not a full new due-diligence review, unless more than 12 months have passed since the last review.

## 5. Contracts

All vendor contracts must be reviewed by Legal before signature, even for a vendor that has passed due diligence — due diligence assesses the vendor, not the specific contract terms.

## 6. Who Can Sign

Only individuals with documented signing authority (typically Department Head and above, or Finance for procurement-specific agreements) may sign a vendor contract on behalf of the company; no individual contributor or manager may sign, regardless of the purchase value.

## 7. Paying the Vendor

Invoices are paid through Finance's standard accounts payable process, referencing the approved purchase order — invoices without a corresponding approved PO may be delayed pending retroactive approval.

## 8. Ending a Vendor Relationship

Notify Finance and Legal before terminating a vendor contract, to check notice period obligations and any data return/deletion requirements under the Data Privacy & GDPR Guidelines.
