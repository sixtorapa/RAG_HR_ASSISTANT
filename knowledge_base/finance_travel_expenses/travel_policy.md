# Travel Policy

## 1. Booking Travel

All business travel is booked through the approved travel platform, which applies pre-negotiated rates. Booking outside the platform is only allowed when the platform genuinely cannot accommodate the trip, and requires manager approval in advance.

## 2. Approval Thresholds

| Trip Type | Approval Needed |
| --- | --- |
| Domestic, under €500 total | Self-booked, manager notified |
| International, or over €500 total | Manager pre-approval |
| Any trip for a non-employee (candidate, contractor) | Manager + Finance pre-approval |

## 3. Flight Class

Economy class for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is permitted for flights over 6 hours; business class requires Department Head approval and is reserved for flights over 9 hours or specific medical accommodation needs (see Accessibility & Accommodations Policy).

## 4. Accommodation

Book within the platform's recommended rate for the destination city; exceeding the recommended rate by more than 20% requires a brief justification note (e.g., conference venue pricing) attached to the booking.

## 5. Ground Transportation

Public transport or ride-share is preferred over rental cars where practical. Rental cars are reimbursable when public transport genuinely isn't a reasonable option (e.g., visiting multiple sites in one day in an area with poor transit).

## 6. High-Risk Destinations

Travel to destinations flagged as higher risk by our travel security provider requires additional sign-off from the People Director and a check-in arrangement with your manager for the duration of the trip (see Workplace Health & Safety Policy).

## 7. Combining Business and Personal Travel

You may extend a business trip for personal travel at your own cost; the company only covers the cost that would have been incurred for the business portion alone (e.g., the cheaper of the business-only fare vs. the actual booked fare).

## 8. Expensing the Trip

All trip-related costs are submitted through the Expense Reimbursement Guide, referencing the original trip approval.
