# Budget Request Process

## 1. Annual Budget Cycle

Department budgets (including team L&D, ERG, and discretionary spend) are set annually each November for the following calendar year, aligned with the Salary Review Calendar's budget envelope process.

## 2. Submitting a Department Budget Request

Department Heads submit a budget request to Finance in October, covering headcount plan, expected team spend categories (travel, software, L&D, vendor contracts), and growth assumptions. Finance reviews against company-wide targets before approving.

## 3. Mid-Year Adjustments

A department can request a mid-year budget adjustment (typically in June) if circumstances have genuinely changed (e.g., unplanned headcount growth, a new strategic project). This requires written justification and Finance approval — it is not automatic.

## 4. Unbudgeted Spend

Any spend not anticipated in the approved budget (a new vendor, an unplanned hire) needs a specific exception approval from Finance, even if the department's overall budget has room, since budgets are tracked by category, not just total.

## 5. Tracking Spend Against Budget

Department Heads can see real-time spend-vs-budget in the Finance dashboard, broken down by category (payroll, travel, software, vendor, L&D). Finance sends a monthly summary flagging any category trending over budget.

## 6. Underspending

Underspend does not roll over to the following year by default; a Department Head who wants to carry forward a specific underspend (e.g., a delayed hire's budget) must request this explicitly as part of the next annual cycle.

## 7. Capital vs. Operating Expense

Equipment purchases over €5,000 per item may be treated as capital expenditure rather than operating expense, which has different budget and accounting treatment — check with Finance before assuming a large purchase fits within a normal operating budget line.

## 8. Questions

Budget category definitions and approval thresholds questions go to Finance; whether a specific department has room in its current budget is a Department Head question first.
