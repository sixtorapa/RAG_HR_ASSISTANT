# Expense Reimbursement Guide

## 1. What's Reimbursable

Reasonable, business-related costs: travel (per the Travel Policy), client meals, L&D spend within your budget (see L&D Annual Budget & Process), gym allowance, and approved equipment purchases.

## 2. Submission Process

Submit expenses through the expense platform within **30 days** of the cost being incurred, with a clear receipt attached. Expenses submitted after 90 days are not guaranteed reimbursement without a specific exception from Finance.

## 3. Approval Flow

| Amount | Approval |
| --- | --- |
| Under €100 | Auto-approved if within a pre-approved category (e.g., gym, L&D under budget) |
| €100-€500 | Manager approval |
| Over €500 | Manager + Finance approval |

## 4. Meal Allowances While Traveling

| Meal | Allowance (where not included in hotel/per diem) |
| --- | --- |
| Breakfast | €15 |
| Lunch | €20 |
| Dinner | €35 |

See Per Diem Rates by Region for destinations where a flat per diem applies instead of itemized meals.

## 5. Required Documentation

A receipt is required for any single expense over €25. For smaller recurring items (e.g., daily transit), a monthly summary is acceptable instead of individual receipts.

## 6. Non-Reimbursable Items

Personal entertainment, alcohol beyond a reasonable amount with a client meal, fines (parking, speeding), and any item already covered by another allowance (e.g., claiming gym costs both here and against the Wellness Program).

## 7. Corporate Card vs. Personal Card

Employees with a corporate card (see Corporate Card Policy) should use it for eligible business expenses rather than paying personally and seeking reimbursement, to simplify reconciliation.

## 8. Disputed or Rejected Expenses

If an expense is rejected, the platform shows the reason; you can resubmit with additional context or escalate to Finance directly if you believe the rejection was made in error.
