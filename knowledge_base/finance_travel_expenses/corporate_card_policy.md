# Corporate Card Policy

## 1. Who Gets a Card

Employees who travel frequently (estimated 4+ trips/year) or manage a team budget are eligible for a corporate card, requested via Finance with manager approval.

## 2. What It's For

Business expenses only: travel, client entertainment, approved software/tools, and team budget purchases (e.g., team L&D events). It is not a personal card and should never be used for personal purchases, even temporarily.

## 3. Spending Limits

| Cardholder Type | Monthly Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Individual contributor (travel-heavy role) | €2,000 |
| Manager (team budget holder) | €5,000 |
| Department Head | €10,000 |

Limits can be temporarily raised for a specific event (e.g., hosting a large team offsite) with Finance approval in advance.

## 4. Reconciliation

All corporate card transactions must be reconciled in the expense platform within **7 days** of the transaction, with a receipt and category attached — this is stricter than the 30-day window for personal reimbursement claims, since unreconciled card spend is harder to audit.

## 5. Lost or Stolen Cards

Report immediately to Finance and the card provider's 24/7 hotline; a replacement is issued within 5 business days.

## 6. Accidental Personal Use

If you accidentally use the corporate card for a personal expense, flag it in the next reconciliation and repay it through payroll deduction or direct transfer — this is treated as an honest mistake if self-reported promptly, not a Code of Conduct violation.

## 7. Card Review on Role Change or Departure

Spending limits are reviewed on promotion or role change. Cards are deactivated immediately as part of standard offboarding on an employee's last working day.

## 8. Repeated Misuse

Repeated personal use, or use without timely reconciliation, may result in the card being revoked and is reviewed under the Disciplinary Procedure if it appears deliberate rather than accidental.
