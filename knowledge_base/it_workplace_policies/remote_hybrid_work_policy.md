# Remote & Hybrid Work Policy

## 1. Standard Arrangement

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week, with core hours of 10:00-16:00 expected regardless of location. Teams agree their specific in-office days together, coordinated by the manager, rather than fully individual schedules.

## 2. Fully Remote Roles

Some roles are designated fully remote at the time of hiring, with no expectation of regular office attendance beyond occasional team gatherings (typically 2-4 times per year, travel-funded under the Travel Policy).

## 3. Working From Abroad

Employees may work from another country temporarily (up to 20 working days per year) with manager approval, subject to confirming there are no tax, legal, or right-to-work complications for that specific country — check with People Ops before booking travel for this purpose.

## 4. Equipment for Remote Work

Standard equipment (laptop, basic peripherals) is provided by IT; the home office stipend (see Benefits Enrollment Guide) covers additional setup. See the Equipment & BYOD Policy for what can be requested.

## 5. Expectations While Remote

- Be reachable during core hours via Slack/calendar status
- Attend scheduled meetings with video on, where the meeting culture expects it
- Maintain the same security practices as in-office (see IT Security & Acceptable Use Policy), including not working on unsecured public Wi-Fi for sensitive tasks without VPN

## 6. Changing Your Arrangement

If you want to move from hybrid to fully remote (or vice versa) on an ongoing basis, discuss with your manager — this is a role/team fit conversation, not an automatic right, since some roles genuinely require more in-person collaboration.

## 7. Underperformance and Remote Work

Remote work privileges are not automatically restricted for performance reasons, but a manager may, as part of a Performance Improvement Plan, require more in-office time if that's identified as a relevant support measure — not used as an implicit punishment.
