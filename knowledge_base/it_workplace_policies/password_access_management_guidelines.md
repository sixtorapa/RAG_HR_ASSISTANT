# Password & Access Management Guidelines

## 1. Password Requirements

- Minimum 14 characters, managed via the approved password manager rather than memorized
- No reuse of passwords across company and personal accounts
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) mandatory on all systems that support it

## 2. Requesting Access

Access to a system is requested through the IT Request Portal, specifying the business reason. Standard access for your role is provisioned automatically as part of the New Hire IT Setup Checklist; anything beyond the standard profile needs manager approval.

## 3. Principle of Least Privilege

You should only have access to the systems and data needed for your current role — having broader access "just in case" is not a valid reason and may be removed during periodic access reviews.

## 4. Periodic Access Reviews

Access to sensitive systems (HRIS, Finance systems, production systems) is reviewed quarterly by the relevant system owner; access not used in the last 90 days is flagged for removal unless there's a documented reason to keep it.

## 5. Access on Role Change

When you change roles (promotion, internal transfer), your access is reviewed and adjusted — old-role access is not automatically kept "just in case," per the Internal Mobility & Transfer Policy.

## 6. Shared Accounts

Shared or generic accounts (e.g., a shared social media login) are discouraged; where unavoidable, they must be registered with IT Security, password-rotated quarterly, and never used by an individual for anything traceable to them personally.

## 7. Offboarding

All access is revoked on an employee's last working day, coordinated automatically between HRIS and IT as part of standard offboarding — see the Employee Lifecycle & HRIS Guide.

## 8. Reporting Suspicious Access

If you notice access you don't believe you should have, or evidence someone else may have accessed your account, report it to IT Security immediately under the IT Security & Acceptable Use Policy.
