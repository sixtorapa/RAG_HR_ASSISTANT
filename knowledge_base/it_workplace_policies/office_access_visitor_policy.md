# Office Access & Visitor Policy

## 1. Employee Badge Access

Office access is via a personal badge, provisioned on your first day and tied to your employment status — access is automatically deactivated on your last working day.

## 2. Office Hours

Badge access is available 24/7 for employees, though out-of-hours access (before 7:00 or after 21:00, or weekends) should have a genuine work reason; unusual access patterns may be reviewed by Facilities as a security measure, not a productivity check.

## 3. Hosting Visitors

All visitors (candidates, vendors, clients, friends/family) must be registered in advance through the Visitor Portal, or signed in at reception on arrival. The host employee is responsible for the visitor for the duration of their visit, including during a fire evacuation (see Emergency Evacuation Procedure).

## 4. Visitor Access Levels

Visitors receive a temporary badge restricted to public areas and meeting rooms; they may not access work areas unaccompanied. Vendors requiring extended or recurring access (e.g., a contractor working on-site for weeks) get a longer-term visitor badge approved by Facilities.

## 5. Lost or Stolen Badges

Report a lost badge immediately to Facilities so it can be deactivated; a replacement is issued at no cost for the first loss in a 12-month period, with a small administrative charge for subsequent losses.

## 6. Tailgating

Do not hold the door for someone without a badge, even if they look familiar or claim to be an employee — direct them to reception. This is a standard physical security practice, not personal rudeness.

## 7. Shared Spaces and Meeting Rooms

Meeting rooms are booked through the calendar system; please release a room promptly if you finish early, since over-booking is a common source of friction across departments.

## 8. Security Concerns

Any concern about an unfamiliar person in the office without a visible badge should be reported to reception or Facilities immediately, rather than confronted directly.
