# IT Security & Acceptable Use Policy

## 1. Scope

Applies to all company-provided devices, accounts, and any personal device used to access company systems (see Equipment & BYOD Policy).

## 2. Account Security

- Use a unique, strong password for your company account, managed via the approved password manager
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory on all systems that support it — see Password & Access Management Guidelines
- Never share your credentials with anyone, including IT support (IT will never ask for your password)

## 3. Acceptable Use

Company systems are for business use, with reasonable personal use permitted (checking personal email, a quick search). Prohibited uses include: installing unauthorized software, accessing illegal content, running personal commercial activity, or bypassing security controls (e.g., disabling antivirus).

## 4. Email and Phishing

Be cautious of unexpected attachments, links, or requests for credentials/payments, even if they appear to come from a colleague or executive. Report suspected phishing using the "Report Phishing" button rather than just deleting it, so IT Security can warn others.

## 5. Public Wi-Fi and VPN

When working from a public or untrusted network, connect via the company VPN before accessing any internal system. Avoid handling sensitive data (personal data, financial data) on public Wi-Fi even with VPN, where reasonably avoidable.

## 6. Data Handling

Company data, especially personal data covered by the Data Privacy & GDPR Guidelines, should only be stored in approved systems (company cloud drive, approved SaaS tools) — not on personal cloud storage, personal email, or personal USB drives.

## 7. Reporting a Security Incident

Suspected security incidents (lost device, suspected malware, accidental data exposure) must be reported to IT Security immediately, and within 24 hours at the latest — the same urgency as a data breach under the Data Privacy & GDPR Guidelines, since many security incidents are also privacy incidents.

## 8. Consequences

Violations of this policy are handled under the Disciplinary Procedure; severity depends on intent and impact — an honest mistake reported quickly is treated very differently from a deliberate or concealed violation.

## 9. Annual Training

All employees complete IT Security & Acceptable Use training within their first week and annually thereafter via the LMS.
