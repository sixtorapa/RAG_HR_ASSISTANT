# Equipment & BYOD Policy

## 1. Standard Equipment

Every employee receives a company laptop (choice of two standard models, requested via the New Hire IT Setup Checklist or, for existing employees, the IT Request Portal on a 3-year refresh cycle).

## 2. Additional Equipment

External monitor, keyboard, mouse, and headset can be requested at no cost against your stipend — see the Ergonomics & Home Office Guidelines. Requests outside this standard list (e.g., a second monitor) need manager approval.

## 3. Bring Your Own Device (BYOD)

Personal phones may be used to access company email and Slack via the approved mobile management profile, which enforces a passcode and allows the company to remotely wipe company data (not personal data) if the device is lost. Personal laptops may not be used to access systems containing personal data or source code, regardless of management profile.

## 4. Equipment Care

Equipment is company property. Accidental damage is covered for repair or replacement at company cost; loss or damage due to clear negligence (e.g., leaving a laptop visibly unattended in a public place) may require a partial contribution from the employee, assessed case-by-case with IT and People Ops.

## 5. Returning Equipment

All company equipment must be returned within 5 business days of your last working day, using the prepaid return process for remote employees. Final pay or final expense reimbursements may be held pending equipment return if not arranged.

## 6. Lost or Stolen Devices

Report immediately to IT Security (see IT Security & Acceptable Use Policy) so the device can be remotely wiped and access revoked, regardless of the time of day.

## 7. Personal Use of Company Equipment

Reasonable personal use is fine (see IT Security & Acceptable Use Policy); company equipment should not be used for a side business or shared with family members.

## 8. Software Requests

New software (beyond the standard build) is requested through the Software Request & License Policy, not directly installed by the employee, to maintain security and licensing compliance.
