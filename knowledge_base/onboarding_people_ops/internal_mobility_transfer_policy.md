# Internal Mobility & Transfer Policy

## 1. Eligibility

Employees become eligible to apply for internal roles after **6 months** in their current role, except in the case of a team-initiated move (e.g., your team is being restructured), which has no minimum tenure requirement.

## 2. How to Apply

Internal roles are posted on the internal careers page alongside external postings, clearly marked "internal." Apply directly through the ATS; you do not need your current manager's permission to apply, though they will be notified once you reach the interview stage.

## 3. Process Differences vs External Candidates

Internal candidates skip the recruiter screen and background check (already on file) but go through the same skills assessment and values interview as external candidates, to keep the bar consistent.

## 4. Notifying Your Current Manager

Talent Acquisition notifies your current manager when you reach the interview stage, not at application. This is to protect your ability to explore opportunities without an awkward early conversation if it doesn't go further.

## 5. Transition Timeline

If selected, the standard transition window is **2-4 weeks**, agreed between your current and future manager, to allow for knowledge handover. Critical-path projects may extend this to a maximum of 6 weeks, but should not be used to indefinitely delay a transfer.

## 6. Compensation on Transfer

A lateral transfer (same level) does not automatically change salary. A transfer to a higher level follows the same band rules as the Promotion & Career Ladder Framework.

## 7. Returning to Your Original Team

If an internal transfer doesn't work out within the first 3 months, the employee can request to return to their original team if a suitable role is available; this is handled case-by-case with People Ops and is not guaranteed.
