# Probation Period Policy

## 1. Duration

All new hires, regardless of level, serve a probation period of **3 months** from their start date. The probation period may be extended once, by up to 3 months, if more time is needed to assess fit — this requires a documented reason from the manager.

## 2. Purpose

Probation is a mutual assessment window: it allows the company to confirm the new hire is performing as expected, and allows the new hire to confirm the role matches expectations set during hiring.

## 3. Check-ins During Probation

| Milestone | Activity |
| --- | --- |
| 2 weeks | Informal check-in with manager |
| 6 weeks | Mid-probation written feedback |
| 12 weeks | Formal probation review and confirmation decision |

## 4. Confirmation

At the end of probation, the manager submits a confirmation decision: **confirmed**, **extended**, or **not confirmed**. Confirmation is the default expected outcome; extension or non-confirmation should not come as a surprise given the milestone check-ins.

## 5. Notice Period During Probation

Notice periods during probation are shorter than standard: **2 weeks** for either party, compared to the standard 1-3 months notice (by level) that applies after confirmation. This is stated explicitly in the employment contract.

## 6. Benefits During Probation

New hires receive full benefits from day one (see Benefits Enrollment Guide) — probation status does not delay or reduce benefits eligibility.

## 7. What Happens If Not Confirmed

If an employee is not confirmed at the end of probation, this is treated as a standard termination during probation, not a disciplinary action, and does not require the Disciplinary Procedure or a PIP. The employee receives written reasoning and any statutory entitlements applicable in their country of employment.
