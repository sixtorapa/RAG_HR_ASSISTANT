# New Hire IT Setup Checklist

## 1. Before Day One (Owned by People Ops + IT)

- [ ] Laptop ordered and configured (see Equipment & BYOD Policy for device options)
- [ ] Company email and SSO account created
- [ ] Access requests submitted for role-required systems (see Password & Access Management Guidelines)
- [ ] Slack/communication tools account provisioned
- [ ] Welcome email sent with first-day logistics

## 2. Day One

- [ ] Laptop handed over (in-office) or shipped with tracked delivery (remote), arriving no later than the day before start date
- [ ] IT orientation session: password setup, VPN, 2FA enrollment
- [ ] Access to HRIS / payroll self-service confirmed
- [ ] Calendar invites for first-week onboarding sessions sent

## 3. First Week

- [ ] Confirm access to all systems listed in the role's access profile
- [ ] Complete mandatory IT Security & Acceptable Use Policy acknowledgment
- [ ] Set up multi-factor authentication on all required systems
- [ ] Join relevant Slack channels and distribution lists

## 4. Remote-Specific Steps

Remote new hires additionally receive the home office stipend information (see Benefits Enrollment Guide) and a checklist for the Ergonomics & Home Office Guidelines, to be completed within the first 30 days.

## 5. Who Owns What

| Step | Owner |
| --- | --- |
| Equipment ordering and shipping | IT |
| Account and access provisioning | IT, triggered by People Ops |
| Welcome logistics and schedule | People Ops |
| Manager introduction and team plan | Hiring Manager |

## 6. Escalation

If equipment or access is not ready by day one, the new hire's manager should escalate directly to IT support with priority flag "new hire blocked" — this is treated as a same-day priority.

## 7. Offboarding Mirror

All access granted here is revoked automatically on the employee's last working day as part of the standard offboarding process — see the Employee Lifecycle & HRIS Guide.
