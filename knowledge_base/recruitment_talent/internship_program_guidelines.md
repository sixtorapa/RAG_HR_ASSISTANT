# Internship Program Guidelines

## 1. Program Overview

Internships run twice a year: a **summer cohort** (June–August, 12 weeks) and a **winter cohort** (January–March, 10 weeks). All internships are paid; we do not offer unpaid internships in any country of operation.

## 2. Eligibility

Open to students currently enrolled in a degree program, or within 6 months of graduation. Each intern is assigned a named mentor (a Senior-level or above employee) and a structured project with a defined deliverable for the cohort.

## 3. Compensation

| Level | Monthly Stipend |
| --- | --- |
| Undergraduate | €1,400 |
| Graduate / Master's | €1,800 |

Interns are not eligible for the bonus or ESOP programs, but do receive the meal allowance and remote-work flexibility on the same terms as full-time employees.

## 4. Conversion to Full-Time

Interns are evaluated at the midpoint and end of their internship using the same scorecard criteria as the standard interview process. Strong performers may receive a full-time offer at the level appropriate to their experience, contingent on headcount availability in their team at the time.

## 5. Mentor Responsibilities

Mentors commit to a weekly 1:1 with their intern and a written mid-point and final evaluation. Mentoring an intern counts toward a Senior employee's leadership development goals (see Leadership Development Track).

## 6. Onboarding

Interns follow a condensed version of the standard Onboarding Guide, completed within their first 3 days rather than the standard first week, given the shorter overall tenure.

## 7. Capacity Planning

Each team may host a maximum of 2 interns per cohort without additional approval; hosting more requires Department Head sign-off to ensure mentor capacity isn't overstretched.
