# Interview Process & Scorecards Guide

## 1. Standard Interview Stages

| Stage | Owner | Typical Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recruiter screen | Talent Acquisition | 30 minutes |
| Hiring manager interview | Hiring manager | 45 minutes |
| Skills assessment | Panel (2-3 people) | 60-90 minutes |
| Values / culture interview | Cross-functional peer | 45 minutes |
| Final interview | Department head | 30 minutes |

Roles at Lead level and above add a final "leadership panel" stage before an offer is extended.

## 2. Scorecards

Every interviewer must submit a scorecard in the ATS within 24 hours of the interview, before discussing the candidate with anyone else on the panel. Scorecards use a 4-point scale:

- **4 — Strong yes**: would be disappointed if we don't hire
- **3 — Yes**: solid hire, no major concerns
- **2 — No**: would not hire, but not a strong objection
- **1 — Strong no**: significant concern about role fit or values

## 3. Debrief Meetings

Debriefs happen within 2 business days of the final interview. The hiring manager facilitates; the most junior panelist speaks first to avoid anchoring bias. A candidate needs at least three "3" or higher scores, with no "1," to proceed to an offer.

## 4. Structured Questions

Each role has a structured question bank maintained by Talent Acquisition. Interviewers should use at least 80% structured questions per interview to keep evaluation criteria consistent across candidates. Ad hoc questions are allowed but should not replace the structured set.

## 5. Avoiding Bias

- Review the CV only immediately before the interview, not days in advance
- Do not discuss scores with other panelists before submitting your own
- Flag and disregard any question about age, marital status, religion, or family planning — these must never factor into a hiring decision

## 6. Candidate Feedback

Rejected candidates who reached the skills assessment stage or beyond receive specific, constructive feedback within 5 business days, drafted by the hiring manager and reviewed by Talent Acquisition before sending.

Sources: Recruitment and Hiring Policy, Hiring Manager Toolkit.
