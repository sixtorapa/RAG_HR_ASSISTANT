# Employee Referral Program

## 1. How It Works

Refer a candidate through the Referrals Portal before they apply elsewhere. If they are hired and complete 3 months of employment, you receive a **€1,000 referral bonus**, paid in the next payroll cycle after their 3-month anniversary.

## 2. Eligible Roles

All open roles posted on the careers page are eligible for referral, except roles at Manager level and above, which carry an increased bonus of **€2,000** due to the higher search cost typically involved.

## 3. Who Can Refer

All current employees, except members of the Talent Acquisition team (referrals are part of their normal job) and anyone directly involved in the hiring decision for that specific role (to avoid conflicts of interest — see the Conflict of Interest Policy).

## 4. Submission Rules

- The candidate must not have an open or recently closed (within 6 months) application for the same role already in the ATS
- You must submit the referral **before** the candidate's first interview; referrals submitted after an interview has occurred are not eligible
- You may refer multiple candidates for the same or different roles, with no cap on total referral bonuses per year

## 5. Tax Treatment

Referral bonuses are paid through payroll and are subject to standard income tax withholding, same as your regular salary.

## 6. Why This Matters

Referred candidates have historically had a 20% higher offer-acceptance rate and a 15% higher one-year retention rate than candidates sourced through other channels, making this one of the most cost-effective ways to find good hires.

## 7. Reporting an Issue

If a referral bonus is not paid within the expected cycle, raise it with People Ops via the HR portal, referencing the candidate's name and your referral submission date.
