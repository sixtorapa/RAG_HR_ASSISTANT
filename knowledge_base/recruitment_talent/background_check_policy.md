# Background Check & Pre-Employment Policy

## 1. When Checks Happen

Background checks are run only **after** a verbal offer has been accepted, never before, and are a condition of final employment. The offer letter explicitly states that the position is contingent on a satisfactory result.

## 2. What Is Checked

| Check Type | Applies To |
| --- | --- |
| Identity verification | All roles |
| Right-to-work verification | All roles |
| Employment history (last 2 employers) | All roles |
| Education verification | Roles requiring a specific degree |
| Criminal record check | Finance, Legal, and roles with system admin access |
| Credit check | Finance roles handling payments only |

## 3. Provider and Consent

Checks are performed by an accredited third-party provider. Candidates must give explicit written consent before any check is initiated; refusal to consent means the offer cannot proceed, since the check is a condition of employment.

## 4. Handling Adverse Findings

If a check returns an adverse finding, the candidate is given the opportunity to explain or dispute it before any decision is made. The decision to withdraw an offer based on a finding is made jointly by the Hiring Manager, People Director, and Legal — never by a single individual.

## 5. Data Retention

Background check results are retained for 12 months after the hiring decision, then deleted, in line with the Data Privacy & GDPR Guidelines. Results are never shared outside the hiring team and Legal.

## 6. International Hires

For candidates outside the company's primary country of operation, checks are adapted to what is legally permissible in that jurisdiction; some check types (e.g., credit checks) are not available or not legal in all countries and are skipped accordingly.

## 7. Re-checks for Internal Moves

Internal employees moving into a role with a higher check requirement (e.g., moving into Finance) undergo the additional check relevant to the new role before the transfer is finalized.
