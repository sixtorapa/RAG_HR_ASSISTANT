# Hiring Manager Toolkit

## 1. Opening a Requisition

Submit a requisition in the ATS with: role title, level, team, budget approval reference, and a draft job description. Requisitions above the approved headcount plan require Finance sign-off before Talent Acquisition will source candidates.

## 2. Writing the Job Description

Use the structured template in the ATS. Avoid gendered language (use tools like the built-in bias checker before publishing) and always include the salary band, per the Pay Equity & Compensation Philosophy.

## 3. Sourcing Timeline Expectations

| Role Level | Typical Time-to-Offer |
| --- | --- |
| Junior / Mid | 3-4 weeks |
| Senior | 4-6 weeks |
| Lead | 6-8 weeks |
| Manager+ | 8-12 weeks |

These are medians, not guarantees; niche skill sets or remote-restricted roles typically take longer.

## 4. Your Responsibilities as Hiring Manager

- Confirm interview panel within 48 hours of a candidate being scheduled
- Submit your own scorecard before discussing the candidate with the panel (see Interview Process & Scorecards Guide)
- Make the final hire/no-hire call after the debrief — Talent Acquisition facilitates but does not decide
- Personally call the candidate to extend a verbal offer; written offers follow from Talent Acquisition

## 5. Making the Offer

Offers must stay within the published band ceiling for the level (see Pay Equity & Compensation Philosophy). Any exception requires Compensation Committee approval before being communicated to the candidate — never promise an off-band number verbally pending approval.

## 6. Closing a Requisition

A requisition closes automatically when an offer is accepted. If you no longer need the role, close it manually in the ATS so candidates in the pipeline can be notified and the role is removed from the careers page.

## 7. First 90 Days

Once hired, your new employee's onboarding plan is owned jointly by you and People Ops — see the Onboarding Guide and the New Hire IT Setup Checklist for what needs to happen before day one.
