# Code of Conduct

## 1. Our Standard

All employees are expected to act with integrity, respect, and professionalism, both internally and when representing the company externally.

## 2. Prohibited Behaviours

- Harassment or bullying of any kind
- Discrimination based on gender, age, race, religion, disability, sexual orientation, or any other protected characteristic
- Falsifying records, expenses, or performance data
- Misuse of company assets or confidential information for personal gain

## 3. Conflicts of Interest

Any situation where your personal interests (financial, family, or otherwise) could influence — or appear to influence — a business decision must be disclosed; see the Conflict of Interest Policy for the disclosure process.

## 4. Gifts and Hospitality

Gifts or hospitality from vendors, clients, or candidates above €100 in value must be declined or, if declining would be awkward, disclosed to your manager and logged with Legal. Cash or cash-equivalent gifts are never acceptable, regardless of value.

## 5. Use of Company Resources

Company equipment, systems, and paid time are provided for business use. Reasonable personal use (a quick personal email, a brief call) is fine; using company resources for an outside business, political campaigning, or anything illegal is not.

## 6. Speaking on Behalf of the Company

Only designated spokespeople (Communications, Legal, or explicitly authorized leadership) may speak to media or make public statements on the company's behalf. Personal social media use is your own, but should not claim to represent official company positions.

## 7. Reporting a Violation

Violations can be reported to your manager, People Ops, or anonymously via the Ethics Hotline. All reports are investigated under the Workplace Investigations Policy and may result in disciplinary action under the Disciplinary Procedure, up to and including termination.

## 8. Retaliation

Retaliation against anyone for reporting a genuine concern in good faith is itself a serious violation of this Code, even if the underlying concern turns out to be unfounded.
