# Whistleblower Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy protects anyone who, in good faith, reports suspected illegal activity, fraud, safety violations, or serious breaches of company policy — even if the report later turns out to be mistaken, as long as it was made honestly.

## 2. The Ethics Hotline

The Ethics Hotline is operated by an independent third party, available 24/7, by phone or web form, and allows fully anonymous submissions. It is not monitored by People Ops directly — reports route to the Audit Committee and Legal.

## 3. What Can Be Reported

- Financial fraud or accounting irregularities
- Bribery or corruption
- Serious health and safety violations
- Discrimination or harassment (also covered directly by the Anti-Harassment Policy)
- Data privacy violations
- Any attempt to pressure someone into falsifying records

## 4. Protection from Retaliation

No employee will be demoted, dismissed, denied a promotion, or otherwise disadvantaged for making a good-faith report under this policy. Any manager found to have retaliated faces disciplinary action up to termination, regardless of their seniority.

## 5. Confidentiality

Your identity, if disclosed, is shared only with those who strictly need to know to conduct the investigation. Anonymous reports are fully possible and carry equal weight in triggering an investigation.

## 6. What Happens After a Report

The Audit Committee or Legal acknowledges receipt within 5 business days (where contact details are provided) and determines whether a formal investigation under the Workplace Investigations Policy is warranted.

## 7. Knowingly False Reports

This protection covers good-faith reports, even if mistaken. It does not protect someone who knowingly makes a false report to harm a colleague — that itself is a Code of Conduct violation.

## 8. External Reporting

Nothing in this policy limits your legal right to report suspected illegal activity directly to a relevant external regulator or authority.
