# Data Privacy & GDPR Guidelines for Employees

## 1. Scope

These guidelines apply to any personal data you handle as part of your job — employee data, candidate data, or customer/client data — regardless of which country you are based in, since we apply GDPR-equivalent standards globally as our baseline.

## 2. Core Principles

- Collect only the personal data you actually need for the task ("data minimization")
- Use it only for the purpose it was collected for
- Keep it only as long as necessary (see retention notes below)
- Keep it secure — see the IT Security & Acceptable Use Policy

## 3. Examples of Personal Data You May Handle

CVs and candidate data, employee records, performance review notes, health-related information (e.g., sick leave reasons), and any customer data depending on your role.

## 4. Retention Periods (Examples)

| Data Type | Retention |
| --- | --- |
| Rejected candidate data | 12 months, then deleted (see Background Check Policy) |
| Former employee HR records | 5 years post-departure, per local legal requirements |
| Incident reports | 3 years |
| Background check results | 12 months |

## 5. Data Subject Rights

Employees and candidates can request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal data. Any such request received by you personally (rather than through the official portal) should be forwarded to Legal within 48 hours — do not attempt to handle it yourself informally.

## 6. Data Breaches

If you suspect a data breach (e.g., a misdirected email with personal data, a lost device with access to personal data), report it immediately to IT Security and Legal — within 24 hours wherever possible, since regulators in many jurisdictions require notification within strict deadlines.

## 7. Sharing Data Externally

Personal data should never be shared with external parties (recruiters, vendors, press) without going through Legal first, even if the request seems routine.

## 8. Training

All employees complete Data Privacy & GDPR Basics training within their first 30 days and annually thereafter via the LMS.
