# Anti-Harassment & Anti-Discrimination Policy

## 1. Zero Tolerance

We have zero tolerance for harassment or discrimination based on gender, age, race, ethnicity, religion, disability, sexual orientation, gender identity, pregnancy, or any other characteristic protected by law in your country of employment.

## 2. What Counts as Harassment

Harassment includes unwelcome conduct that creates an intimidating, hostile, or offensive environment — this can be verbal, physical, or visual (including messages, images, or jokes), and can come from a manager, peer, client, or vendor.

## 3. Sexual Harassment Specifically

Includes unwelcome sexual advances, requests for favors, and any conduct of a sexual nature that affects someone's working conditions, whether explicit ("quid pro quo") or through a pattern of unwelcome behavior creating a hostile environment.

## 4. How to Report

Report to your manager, People Ops, the People Director, or anonymously via the Ethics Hotline — you can choose whichever channel feels safest. You do not need to report to your direct manager if they are the subject of the concern.

## 5. What Happens After a Report

All reports are taken seriously and investigated under the Workplace Investigations Policy, typically starting within 5 business days. Confidentiality is maintained to the extent possible while still allowing a fair investigation for all parties.

## 6. Protection from Retaliation

It is a separate, serious violation to retaliate against someone for reporting in good faith, participating in an investigation as a witness, or supporting a colleague who has reported.

## 7. Outcomes

Substantiated violations result in disciplinary action proportional to severity, ranging from a formal warning to immediate termination, under the Disciplinary Procedure. Unsubstantiated reports made in good faith carry no consequence for the reporter.

## 8. Mandatory Training

All employees complete Anti-Harassment & Anti-Discrimination training within their first 30 days and annually thereafter, via the LMS (see LMS User Guide).
