# Learning Management System (LMS) User Guide

## 1. Accessing the LMS

The LMS is available via single sign-on (SSO) from the internal portal. Access is provisioned automatically on your first day as part of the New Hire IT Setup Checklist.

## 2. Mandatory Courses

| Course | Due Within | Frequency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IT Security & Acceptable Use | First week | Annual refresher |
| Anti-Harassment & Anti-Discrimination | First 30 days | Annual refresher |
| Data Privacy & GDPR Basics | First 30 days | Annual refresher |
| Code of Conduct | First 30 days | Once, plus on major policy updates |

Mandatory course completion is tracked by People Ops and reported to managers monthly; overdue mandatory training is escalated to the manager after a 2-week grace period.

## 3. Optional Learning Paths

The LMS also hosts optional, role-specific learning paths (e.g., management fundamentals, advanced Excel, communication skills) that do not expire and can be taken at your own pace using your individual L&D time, though they don't draw down your L&D budget since the LMS itself is centrally licensed.

## 4. Tracking Progress

Your completion history and certificates are visible under "My Learning" and exportable as a PDF — useful for performance review evidence or external job applications.

## 5. Requesting New Content

If a course you need isn't available, submit a request through the L&D Request Form; the L&D team reviews requests quarterly and may license a new course or build internal content depending on demand.

## 6. Technical Issues

LMS login or video playback issues go to IT support, not the L&D team, since the LMS is a third-party hosted platform.

## 7. Manager Visibility

Managers can see completion status for their direct reports only, not for the wider organization, to keep the data need-to-know.
