# Mentorship Program Guide

## 1. Program Overview

The mentorship program pairs employees seeking growth in a specific area with a mentor who has relevant experience, for a structured 6-month cycle, twice a year (cohorts start in March and September).

## 2. Who Can Be a Mentor

Any employee at Senior level or above with at least 12 months' tenure can volunteer as a mentor. Mentors can support up to 2 mentees per cohort. Mentoring counts toward leadership development goals on the Leadership Development Track.

## 3. Who Can Be a Mentee

Open to all employees. You select up to 3 development areas when applying (e.g., "public speaking," "moving into management," "technical depth in X") and are matched based on mentor availability and expertise.

## 4. The Matching Process

People Ops runs the matching using submitted preferences; matches are not guaranteed to be a perfect fit on the first attempt. You can request a re-match once per cohort if the pairing genuinely isn't working, with a brief note on why.

## 5. Structure of the Relationship

- Recommended cadence: one 45-minute session every 2 weeks
- A short kickoff conversation to agree on goals for the 6 months
- A mid-point and final check-in survey from People Ops to both parties

## 6. This Is Not a Performance Relationship

A mentor is never the mentee's manager and has no input into their Annual Performance Review, compensation, or promotion decisions. Conversations are confidential between mentor and mentee unless the mentee chooses to share specifics elsewhere.

## 7. Ending Early

Either party can end the mentorship early with a short note to People Ops — no justification is required beyond "this isn't the right fit right now."
