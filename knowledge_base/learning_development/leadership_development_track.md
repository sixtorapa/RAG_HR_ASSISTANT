# Leadership Development Track

## 1. Who This Is For

Employees at Senior level who have expressed interest in moving toward Lead or Manager roles, nominated by their manager and confirmed by the Department Head. The track runs as an annual 9-month cohort starting in February.

## 2. What the Track Includes

| Component | Frequency |
| --- | --- |
| Leadership workshops (cohort-based) | Monthly, half-day |
| 1:1 executive coaching | Bi-weekly |
| Stretch project with cross-functional visibility | Once per cohort |
| Mentoring at least one junior colleague or intern | Ongoing |

## 3. Selection Process

Nominations are submitted by managers in December for the following year's cohort. The Promotion Committee reviews nominations alongside recent performance scores and OKR history; selection is not a promise of promotion.

## 4. Cost and Budget

The track is funded centrally by the L&D team budget, separate from individual L&D budgets, so participation does not reduce your personal €1,500 annual allowance.

## 5. Relationship to Promotions

Completing the track does not automatically result in a promotion to Lead — promotions still go through the standard Promotion & Career Ladder Framework and Promotion Committee process. The track is preparation, not a guarantee.

## 6. Time Commitment

Expect roughly 4-6 hours per month outside regular workload. Managers are expected to account for this when setting OKRs for cohort participants during the 9 months.

## 7. If You're Not Selected

Employees not selected receive specific feedback on what would strengthen a future nomination and can reapply in subsequent years; there is no limit on the number of times you can be nominated.
