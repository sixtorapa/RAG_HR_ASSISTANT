# External Training Reimbursement Policy

## 1. Scope

This policy covers reimbursement for external courses, bootcamps, and structured training programs that are not covered by your standard L&D budget process for smaller purchases (see L&D Annual Budget & Process for the €300 self-approval threshold).

## 2. Eligible Training

Training must be clearly relevant to your current role or a documented next-level competency gap (see Promotion & Career Ladder Framework). Purely personal-interest courses unrelated to your role are not eligible, even if within your individual L&D budget.

## 3. Pre-Approval Requirement

Any course costing more than €300, or requiring time off during working hours, requires written pre-approval from your manager before enrollment. Retroactive reimbursement requests for unapproved training are reviewed case-by-case and are not guaranteed.

## 4. Reimbursement Process

Submit the receipt and approval reference through the Expense Reimbursement Guide within 60 days of completing the course. Reimbursement is processed in the next payroll cycle after approval.

## 5. Completion Requirement

For multi-week courses or bootcamps, reimbursement is contingent on completing at least 80% of the program. If you do not complete the course, the company may recover the cost from your final payslip if you also leave the company within 6 months of the training.

## 6. Repayment on Early Departure

If a single training investment exceeds €2,000 and you resign within 12 months of completing it, a pro-rated portion of the cost is repayable to the company, as set out in your training agreement signed at the time of approval.

## 7. Time Off for Training

Training during working hours counts as working time, not annual leave, provided it was pre-approved under this policy.
