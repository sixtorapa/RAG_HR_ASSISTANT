# Learning & Development — Annual Budget & Process

## 1. Individual L&D Budget

Every employee has an individual L&D budget of **€1,500 per year**, resetting each 1 January. Unused budget does not roll over to the following year.

## 2. What the Budget Can Cover

- External courses, certifications, and conferences (see Certifications & Conference Attendance Policy)
- Books and learning subscriptions (e.g., online course platforms)
- Coaching sessions, where relevant to a documented development goal

## 3. What It Cannot Cover

- Equipment or software already covered under the Equipment & BYOD Policy
- Travel and accommodation for conferences (these go through the Travel Policy and Expense Reimbursement Guide, charged to the team budget, not the individual L&D budget)
- Degrees or qualifications unrelated to your current or clearly planned future role

## 4. Approval Process

Spend up to €300 per item can be self-approved through the Expense Reimbursement Guide with manager notification. Spend above €300 requires manager pre-approval via the L&D Request Form before booking.

## 5. Team-Level L&D Budget

In addition to individual budgets, each department has a discretionary team L&D budget (set annually by Finance) for group training, workshops, or bringing in external facilitators — this is separate from and does not reduce individual budgets.

## 6. Tracking Usage

Usage against your individual budget is visible in the Benefits Portal in real time. People Ops sends a reminder in October each year for anyone who hasn't used any of their budget, as a nudge rather than a requirement.

## 7. Linking Spend to Goals

We recommend (but do not require) tying L&D spend to a goal in your OKRs or Annual Performance Review development plan — this makes the value of training clearer in your next review.
