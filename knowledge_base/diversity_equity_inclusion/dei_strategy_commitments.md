# DEI Strategy & Commitments

## 1. Our Commitments

- Achieve and maintain gender balance (40-60% range) at every level by 2027, reviewed annually
- Run an annual Pay Equity Audit and correct any unexplained gap larger than 3% (see Pay Equity Audit Summary)
- Maintain Inclusive Hiring practices across all recruiting (see Inclusive Hiring Guidelines)
- Support at least 3 active Employee Resource Groups (ERGs) with dedicated budget

## 2. Governance

DEI strategy is owned by the People Director and reviewed quarterly by the DEI Council, which includes ERG leads, a People Analytics representative, and one rotating employee seat open to anyone via nomination.

## 3. How Progress Is Measured

| Metric | Reviewed |
| --- | --- |
| Gender/level representation | Quarterly |
| Pay equity gap | Annually (Pay Equity Audit) |
| Hiring funnel diversity (where legally permitted to track) | Quarterly |
| ERG participation and budget utilization | Annually |

## 4. Transparency

A summary of DEI metrics is shared company-wide twice a year in an all-hands; the underlying detailed data stays with the DEI Council and People Analytics to protect individual privacy, especially in smaller teams where aggregate numbers could otherwise identify individuals.

## 5. Manager Accountability

Manager-level DEI awareness (inclusive hiring, recognizing bias in performance calibration) is included in onboarding for new managers and revisited as part of the Leadership Development Track.

## 6. This Is Not a Quota System

Representation targets are aspirational goals tracked at the aggregate level, not quotas applied to individual hiring or promotion decisions — every individual decision is still made strictly on merit per the Promotion & Career Ladder Framework and Inclusive Hiring Guidelines.

## 7. Feedback

Employees can raise DEI-related feedback or concerns to any ERG lead, the People Director, or anonymously via the Ethics Hotline.
