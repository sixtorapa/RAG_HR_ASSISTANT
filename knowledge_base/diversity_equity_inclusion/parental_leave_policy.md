# Parental Leave Policy

## 1. Entitlement

| Role | Leave |
| --- | --- |
| Primary caregiver | 16 weeks, fully paid |
| Secondary caregiver | 6 weeks, fully paid |

This applies equally regardless of gender, and regardless of whether the child arrives by birth, surrogacy, or adoption.

## 2. Eligibility

Available to all employees from their first day of employment — there is no minimum tenure requirement, unlike some other leave types.

## 3. Notice Period

Please notify People Ops at least 8 weeks before your expected leave start date where possible, so coverage can be planned; we understand this isn't always possible (e.g., premature birth) and will work with shorter notice when needed.

## 4. Taking Leave Flexibly

Primary caregiver leave can be split into two blocks within the first 12 months (e.g., 12 weeks immediately, 4 weeks later), with manager agreement. Secondary caregiver leave must be taken within the first 6 months and can be split into a maximum of 2 blocks.

## 5. Pay and Benefits During Leave

Leave is paid at 100% of base salary (not including bonus, which is calculated separately — see Bonus & Incentive Plan FAQ for pro-ration rules). All benefits, including pension contributions, continue uninterrupted during leave.

## 6. Returning to Work

You return to the same role and level, or an equivalent one, with no negative impact on your position in the Promotion & Career Ladder Framework. Performance review timing is adjusted to account for time on leave, so leave does not unfairly affect your score.

## 7. Phased Return

On return, you can request a phased return (e.g., reduced hours for the first 4 weeks at full pay) with manager agreement — this is at the company's discretion and reviewed case by case.

## 8. Pregnancy-Related Leave Before Birth

Pregnancy-related medical appointments and any medically necessary leave before the birth are covered under standard Sick Leave, separate from and in addition to the 16-week primary caregiver entitlement that starts at birth/placement.
