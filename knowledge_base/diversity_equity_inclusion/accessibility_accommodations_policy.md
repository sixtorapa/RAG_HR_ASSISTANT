# Accessibility & Accommodations Policy

## 1. Our Commitment

We provide reasonable accommodations to employees and candidates with disabilities or health conditions, so that they can perform their role and participate fully in the hiring process and workplace.

## 2. Requesting an Accommodation

Contact People Ops or, during hiring, Talent Acquisition. You do not need to disclose a specific diagnosis — describing the functional need (e.g., "I need a flexible start time" or "I need screen-reader-compatible materials") is enough to start the conversation.

## 3. Examples of Accommodations

- Flexible hours or adjusted core-hours expectations
- Ergonomic equipment beyond the standard stipend (see Ergonomics & Home Office Guidelines)
- Accessible interview formats or locations
- Additional, paid time for medical appointments beyond standard sick leave
- Modified duties on a temporary or ongoing basis, agreed with the manager

## 4. Confidentiality

Accommodation requests and any supporting medical information are handled by People Ops only and are not shared with your manager beyond what's needed to implement the accommodation itself (e.g., "needs flexible start time," not the underlying medical reason).

## 5. Approval Timeline

People Ops aims to respond within 5 business days of a request, though some accommodations (e.g., equipment) may take longer to actually arrange — you'll be told the expected timeline once the accommodation is agreed.

## 6. If a Request Is Declined

Very rarely, an accommodation may not be feasible (e.g., it would fundamentally change the nature of the role). In that case, People Ops will explain why and explore alternative accommodations that might meet the same underlying need.

## 7. Office Accessibility

All office locations are reviewed for basic physical accessibility (step-free access, accessible restrooms) as part of the lease selection process; specific gaps can be raised with Facilities for remediation.

## 8. This Applies Throughout Employment

Accommodation needs can change over time (e.g., after an injury, during a health condition) — you can request an adjustment at any point, not just during onboarding.
