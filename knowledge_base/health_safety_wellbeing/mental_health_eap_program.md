# Mental Health & Employee Assistance Program (EAP)

## 1. What's Included

All employees and their household members have access to **8 free therapy sessions per year** via Oliva Health, our mental health partner. Sessions are confidential — the company never receives information about who used the service or what was discussed.

## 2. How to Access

Register directly through the Oliva Health portal using your company email; no manager approval or referral is needed. Sessions can be booked within 48 hours typically, and there is a crisis line available 24/7 for urgent situations.

## 3. Beyond the 8 Sessions

If you need ongoing support beyond the 8 covered sessions, Oliva Health can help you transition to your local healthcare or insurance-covered options; the company's private health insurance (see Benefits Enrollment Guide) may cover additional sessions depending on your country's plan.

## 4. Manager Training

Managers complete a short mental health awareness module (via the LMS) within their first 90 days as a manager, covering how to recognize signs of distress and how to point a team member toward support without overstepping into a clinical role themselves.

## 5. Mental Health and Performance

If a mental health concern is affecting your work, you're encouraged to talk to your manager or People Ops — reasonable, temporary adjustments (reduced hours, deadline flexibility) can often be made without this counting against you in a review cycle. This is handled separately from the Performance Improvement Plan Process where possible.

## 6. Leave for Mental Health

Mental health-related absences are covered under standard Sick Leave (see Employee Handbook) with no separate category or stigma — you do not need to disclose the specific reason for sick leave beyond what your manager needs to plan coverage.

## 7. Confidentiality

People Ops never asks Oliva Health for usage data tied to individuals. Aggregate, anonymized usage statistics are reviewed annually by the Health & Safety Committee purely to evaluate whether the program is being used and is adequately resourced.
