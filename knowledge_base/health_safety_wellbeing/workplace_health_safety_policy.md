# Workplace Health & Safety Policy

## 1. Our Commitment

The company is committed to providing a safe working environment, whether you work from an office, remotely, or a hybrid arrangement, in line with applicable local health and safety law in each country of operation.

## 2. Office Safety

- Fire safety briefings are mandatory for all office-based employees within their first week
- Office first-aid kits and a trained first-aider are present on every floor; their location and name are posted near the entrance
- Any safety hazard (spill, broken equipment, blocked exit) should be reported immediately via the Incident Reporting Procedure

## 3. Remote and Home Office Safety

Remote employees are responsible for maintaining a reasonably safe home workspace. The Ergonomics & Home Office Guidelines provide a self-assessment checklist; the home office stipend (see Benefits Enrollment Guide) can be used toward safety-relevant equipment such as a proper chair.

## 4. Reporting an Incident

All workplace incidents — injuries, near-misses, and hazards — must be reported within 24 hours using the Incident Reporting Procedure, regardless of severity. Near-miss reporting is encouraged and never penalized; it is how we prevent future incidents.

## 5. Health and Safety Representatives

Each office location has a nominated Health & Safety Representative, who sits on the quarterly Health & Safety Committee review alongside People Ops and Facilities.

## 6. Travel Safety

Business travel safety guidance (including high-risk destination protocols) is covered in the Travel Policy; employees traveling to a destination flagged as higher risk must check in with their manager on arrival and departure.

## 7. Right to Refuse Unsafe Work

Any employee can refuse a task they reasonably believe to be unsafe without fear of retaliation, and should report the situation immediately to their manager and the Health & Safety Representative.
