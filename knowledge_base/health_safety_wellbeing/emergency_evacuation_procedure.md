# Emergency Evacuation Procedure

## 1. Scope

This procedure applies to all office locations and covers fire, gas leak, structural concern, or any situation requiring immediate evacuation.

## 2. On Hearing the Alarm

1. Stop work immediately, do not finish "just one more thing"
2. Leave personal belongings behind unless they are within immediate reach
3. Use the nearest marked exit — do not use elevators
4. Proceed to the designated assembly point (posted on the safety notice near each exit)
5. Wait for the floor warden to confirm headcount before leaving the assembly point

## 3. Floor Wardens

Each floor has a designated floor warden and a backup, listed on the safety notice board. Wardens are responsible for sweeping their floor (including meeting rooms and restrooms) and confirming evacuation before reporting to the assembly point lead.

## 4. Visitors and Contractors

Hosts are responsible for guiding their visitors to the assembly point and including them in the headcount — visitors do not know the building layout and should never be left to find their own way out.

## 5. Employees Requiring Assistance

Employees who may need assistance evacuating (mobility, temporary injury) should inform Facilities in advance so a personal emergency evacuation plan (PEEP) can be set up; this is confidential and only shared with the relevant floor warden.

## 6. Drills

Fire drills are run at least twice a year, unannounced in timing but always during core working hours. Participation is mandatory — treat every drill as if it were real.

## 7. After an Evacuation

Do not re-enter the building until explicitly cleared by emergency services or Facilities. Any real evacuation is logged through the Incident Reporting Procedure regardless of cause.
