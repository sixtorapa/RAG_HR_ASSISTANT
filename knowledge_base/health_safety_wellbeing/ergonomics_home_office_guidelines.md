# Ergonomics & Home Office Guidelines

## 1. Self-Assessment Checklist

New remote employees should complete this checklist within their first 30 days:

- [ ] Chair supports lower back, seat height allows feet flat on the floor
- [ ] Monitor (or laptop) top of screen at or just below eye level
- [ ] Keyboard and mouse positioned so elbows stay close to 90 degrees
- [ ] Adequate lighting to avoid eye strain; natural light supplemented if needed
- [ ] Dedicated, reasonably quiet workspace for calls

## 2. Home Office Stipend

The €500 one-time setup payment and €30/month ongoing stipend (see Benefits Enrollment Guide) are intended to be used toward ergonomic equipment: a proper chair, external monitor, keyboard/mouse, or a sit-stand desk converter.

## 3. Company-Provided Equipment

In addition to the stipend, employees can request an external monitor and ergonomic keyboard/mouse directly from IT at no cost against the stipend — see the Equipment & BYOD Policy for the request process.

## 4. Movement and Breaks

We recommend the 50/10 rule: a short 5-10 minute break roughly every 50 minutes, including standing up and looking away from the screen. This is a wellbeing recommendation, not a tracked requirement.

## 5. Reporting Discomfort

If you experience ongoing discomfort or pain you believe is related to your home workspace, report it through the Incident Reporting Procedure as a non-urgent item — this is treated the same as an office ergonomics concern and may trigger additional equipment support beyond the standard stipend.

## 6. Office-Based Employees

Office-based employees can request an ergonomic assessment from Facilities at any time, free of charge, including a sit-stand desk if recommended.

## 7. Re-Assessment

We recommend revisiting this checklist whenever your home setup changes significantly (moving house, changing your primary work room) rather than only once at onboarding.
