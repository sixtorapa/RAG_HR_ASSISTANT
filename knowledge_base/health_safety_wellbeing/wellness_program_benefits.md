# Wellness Program & Benefits

## 1. What's Covered

| Benefit | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Gym allowance | €50/month, reimbursed via expenses |
| Mental health | 8 therapy sessions/year (Oliva Health) |
| Annual health check | Optional, fully covered, once per year |
| Wellness days | 2 additional paid days/year, separate from annual leave |

## 2. Wellness Days

Wellness days are 2 extra paid days per year intended for proactive self-care (not sick leave) — a mental health reset, a medical check-up, or simply rest. They are booked the same way as annual leave but tracked in a separate balance, and do not carry over if unused at year-end.

## 3. Annual Health Check

The optional annual health check is delivered through our insurance partner and includes standard bloodwork and a GP consultation. Results go directly and only to you; the company never receives individual health data, only an anonymized utilization rate for program evaluation.

## 4. Gym Allowance Reimbursement

Submit your gym membership or class-pass receipt monthly through the Expense Reimbursement Guide, tagged "wellness." Home gym equipment is not eligible under this allowance, but may be eligible under the Home Office Stipend if used primarily for desk-based ergonomics rather than general fitness.

## 5. Group Wellness Activities

Each office runs at least one group wellness activity per quarter (e.g., a yoga session, a step challenge), funded by the team L&D/wellness budget, organized locally rather than centrally mandated.

## 6. Eligibility

All employees are eligible from day one, including those on probation — unlike some benefits, wellness benefits are not affected by probation status.

## 7. Questions

Questions about specific reimbursement amounts go to People Ops; clinical questions about the annual health check go to the insurance provider directly.
