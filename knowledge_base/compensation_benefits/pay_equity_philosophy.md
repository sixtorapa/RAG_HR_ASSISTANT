# Pay Equity & Compensation Philosophy

## 1. Our Principle

We pay for role, level, and performance — not for negotiation skill, tenure alone, or background. This document explains how that principle is enforced in practice.

## 2. Market Positioning

We target the **65th percentile** of the market for base salary, benchmarked annually against Radford and Mercer survey data, segmented by role family and country. Bands are reviewed every year as part of the Salary Review Calendar.

## 3. Annual Pay Equity Audit

Every year, the People Analytics team runs a statistical pay equity audit that compares compensation across gender and ethnicity (where legally permitted to collect) within the same level and role family, controlling for tenure and performance score. Any gap larger than 3% that cannot be explained by these factors is flagged for correction in the next review cycle.

## 4. Salary Negotiation Policy

Initial offers are made strictly within the published band for the level and role. Hiring managers and recruiters are not authorized to exceed the band ceiling without sign-off from the Compensation Committee. This is designed to prevent negotiation skill from creating unequal outcomes between candidates.

## 5. Internal Transparency

Employees can see the full salary band for their own level and role family on the internal Compensation Wiki. We do not publish individual salaries, but band ranges and the criteria for moving within a band (see Salary Review Calendar) are fully transparent.

## 6. Pay Transparency by Geography

In jurisdictions where pay transparency is legally required (e.g., salary ranges in job postings), Recruiting publishes the exact band for the advertised level. Where not legally required, we still recommend including the band as a matter of policy.

## 7. Reporting a Concern

If you believe you are being paid unfairly relative to peers in the same role and level, you can raise this confidentially with the People Director or anonymously via the Ethics Hotline. All reports are investigated as part of the next scheduled pay equity audit, or sooner if the concern is urgent.

Sources: Compensation & Benefits Policy, Pay Equity Audit Summary (DEI).
