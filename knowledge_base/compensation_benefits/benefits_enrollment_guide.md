# Benefits Enrollment Guide

## 1. When You Can Enroll

- **New hires**: within your first 14 days, via the Benefits Portal. Coverage starts on your official start date.
- **Annual open enrollment**: every November, for changes effective 1 January.
- **Qualifying life events**: marriage, birth/adoption of a child, or loss of other coverage open a 30-day enrollment window outside the normal cycle.

## 2. Benefits Summary

| Benefit | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Health insurance | Full private cover, employee + 1 dependent |
| Pension | 5% employer match (up to 5% employee contribution) |
| Gym allowance | €50/month, reimbursed via expenses |
| Home office setup | €500 one-time + €30/month stipend |
| Mental health | 8 therapy sessions/year (Oliva Health) |
| Meal allowance | €8/working day (card provided) |
| Transport | €60/month public transport card |

## 3. Adding Dependents

You may add one dependent (spouse, domestic partner, or child) to your health insurance at no additional cost. Adding a second or further dependent is possible at your own cost through the Benefits Portal; pricing is shown at the time of enrollment and varies by age band.

## 4. Opting Out

You may opt out of the company health plan only if you can show proof of equivalent coverage elsewhere (e.g., through a spouse's employer or public healthcare in your country of residence). Opting out does not increase your salary or provide a cash alternative.

## 5. Pension Contribution Changes

You can increase or decrease your voluntary pension contribution (up to the 5% matched maximum) at any time through the Benefits Portal; changes take effect from the following payroll cycle. The employer match never exceeds 5% of base salary regardless of how much you contribute above that level.

## 6. Losing Eligibility

Benefits coverage ends on your last day of employment, except health insurance, which continues until the last day of the month in which you leave. COBRA-equivalent continuation options (where applicable by country) are detailed in your offboarding pack.

## 7. Questions

Enrollment issues and portal access problems go to People Ops. Coverage and claims questions go directly to the insurance provider listed in the Benefits Portal.
