# Salary Review Calendar & Process

Effective January 2025 | Confidential

## 1. Purpose

This document describes the annual timeline and process for salary reviews across all departments. It complements the Compensation & Benefits Policy and should be read alongside the published salary bands.

## 2. Annual Calendar

| Month | Activity |
| --- | --- |
| November | Managers submit performance scores for the cycle |
| December | People team runs market benchmarking (Radford / Mercer) |
| January | Compensation committee reviews budget and band adjustments |
| 1 February | New salaries become effective |
| 15 February | Individual results communicated to employees |

## 3. Who Is Eligible

Employees must be employed continuously since 1 September of the prior year and not currently on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) to be eligible for a review-cycle increase. Employees hired after 1 September are reviewed in the following cycle.

## 4. How Increases Are Calculated

Increases are a function of three inputs:

- **Performance score** (from the Annual Performance Review)
- **Position within band** — employees below the midpoint of their band receive a higher weighting toward market catch-up
- **Department budget envelope** — set by Finance each November as a percentage of total payroll (typically 3–5%)

## 5. Off-Cycle Reviews

Off-cycle increases are only approved for: promotions, retention risk flagged by a manager and approved by the People Director, or correction of a confirmed pay equity gap. Off-cycle requests are submitted via the Compensation Request Form and require two approval levels.

## 6. Communication

Managers receive a calibration pack two weeks before results are communicated. Employees should direct questions about their individual result to their manager first; unresolved questions can be escalated to the People team via the HR portal.

Sources: Compensation Committee Charter, internal benchmarking data (Radford/Mercer).
