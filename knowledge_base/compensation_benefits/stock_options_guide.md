# Stock Options (ESOP) — Detailed Guide

## 1. Who Receives Options

Employees at Senior level and above receive stock options upon joining and at each promotion. Grants are approved by the board quarterly and documented in your grant letter.

## 2. Vesting Schedule

Standard vesting is 4 years with a 1-year cliff:

- 25% vests after year 1 (the "cliff") — nothing vests before this date
- The remaining 75% vests monthly over years 2–4 (roughly 2.08% per month)

## 3. Exercise Window

If you leave the company (voluntarily or involuntarily), you have **90 days** from your last working day to exercise any vested options, after which they expire and return to the option pool. Options that have not vested at your termination date are forfeited immediately.

## 4. Strike Price

The strike price is set at the fair market value (FMV) of the company on the grant date, as determined by the latest independent 409A-equivalent valuation. Strike price does not change after grant, even if the company's valuation later increases or decreases.

## 5. Refresh Grants

Employees who have vested more than 50% of their original grant become eligible for a "refresh grant" at their next performance review, subject to manager nomination and compensation committee approval. Refresh grants follow the same 4-year/1-year-cliff schedule, independent of the original grant's vesting progress.

## 6. Tax Considerations

Tax treatment of options depends on your country of employment and is not advice from the company. Employees are encouraged to consult an independent tax advisor before exercising, particularly around the cliff date or before a liquidity event.

## 7. What Happens on a Liquidity Event

In the event of an acquisition or IPO, the treatment of unvested options (acceleration, conversion, or assumption by the acquirer) is determined by the board at the time of the transaction and communicated to all option holders in writing at least 30 days in advance where possible.

## 8. Common Questions

**Can I sell my shares before an IPO?** No, all option-derived shares are subject to a right of first refusal by the company and a lock-up period defined in your grant agreement.

**Can I exercise early, before vesting?** Only if your grant letter explicitly allows early exercise; this is not standard for grants issued after January 2024.
